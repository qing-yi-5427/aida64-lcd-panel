No text, letters, numbers, watermark or presentation mockup. One centered bold screen-shaped mark with a simple sun/power glow inside, an instantly legible silhouette at 16px. Palette based on the existing app: near-black charcoal and vivid chartreuse lime #C8FF33. Flat premium geometric design, subtle restrained depth, generous clean negative space; a rounded dark square tile filling most of the canvas, with truly transparent pixels outside its rounded corners. Straight front view, single icon only. Output high resolution PNG with transparency suitable for conversion to a multi-resolution Windows ICO.
